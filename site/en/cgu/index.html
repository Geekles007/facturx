2. What these terms cover

With an account, the Studio keeps your drafts, your company, your customers, your catalogue and your numbering on a server. It issues your invoices, tracks the payments you enter, shows a dashboard and lets you send reminders to your customers.

Without an account none of this exists: the Studio keeps everything in your browser, and the online validator reads your file in your browser. Neither sends what you type or drop to any server, and these terms do not apply to them. The only signal that leaves is a content-free count, described in the privacy policy.

5. What the Service does, and what it does not

  • It issues your invoices in the Factur-X format, EN 16931 profile: the server assigns the number, in sequence; the invoice is checked with the facturx-sdk SDK; the PDF is produced and frozen.
  • An issued invoice is neither edited nor deleted while the account exists, by you or by us. To correct a mistake, you issue a credit note. The only exception is the purge of a deleted account (section 9).
  • It works out the payment status (paid, partly paid, overdue…) from the payments you enter. It does not connect to your bank.
  • It does not send your invoices to an accredited platform: this version is not connected to one.
  • It is not legal, tax or accounting advice. You remain responsible for the content of your invoices (your statements, your VAT rates, your customers' details) and for their compliance.

6. Keeping your invoices

The law requires you to keep your invoices for ten years. That obligation is yours, not facturx's.

The Service keeps your invoices for as long as your account exists; when the account is deleted, they are erased (section 9). “Export my data” gives you a zip archive with a portable JSON file and all your PDFs: keep them, for the legal period, on a medium you control.

The Service is free and comes with no commitment on availability. Export your data regularly, without waiting for the account to be deleted.

7. Reminders

A reminder never goes out on its own. “Send a reminder” is offered on an invoice whose due date has passed and which still has an amount to pay. It prepares an e-mail that you read, edit, then send: nothing is sent without that step, and the Service sends no automatic reminders.

  • The e-mail is addressed to your customer's contact address on the invoice, which you can change. The invoice PDF is attached.
  • It is sent from “Your company name via facturx”, on facturx's domain. Your account address is set as the reply address (Reply-To): your customer's replies reach you directly.
  • The suggested text depends on how many reminders have already been sent for that invoice: a friendly reminder, a firm reminder, then a last reminder that cites the late-payment penalties and the €40 fixed recovery fee written on the invoice.
  • The Studio shows “sent”, never “received”: our sending provider accepted the e-mail, and nothing guarantees it was read.

The content of the reminder is yours, and it is up to you to make sure you may contact that customer. Do not use reminders for anything other than collecting your own invoices.

9. Deleting your account

  1. You export first. The Studio takes you through “Export my data” and reminds you that keeping your invoices for ten years is a legal obligation that rests on you.
  2. You request deletion. From then on, the Service refuses to issue invoices or send reminders. Each time you sign in, the Studio offers only the export, “Cancel the deletion” and “Sign out”.
  3. After 30 days, at the first nightly run that follows, everything is erased, in this order: the PDFs and files, your sign-in identity, your company's data (invoices, customers, catalogue, payments, reminders), then the traces of your sign-ins kept by the authentication server. This cannot be undone.

During those 30 days you can cancel the deletion and get your account back as it was. What happens to backup copies and to the host's technical logs is described in the privacy policy.
