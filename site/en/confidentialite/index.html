Privacy policy

What facturx does with your data, depending on whether you use the Studio without an account or with one: what is received, where it is hosted, how long it stays, and what you can demand.

Terms Privacy Data processing

1. Who is responsible

For your account's data, the controller is the publisher of the facturx service, named below. For your customers' data that you enter in the Service, you are the controller and facturx is your processor: see the data processing agreement.

To be completed by the publisher: name or company name, address, SIREN number, contact address, effective date of version 2026-11.

This policy carries version number 2026-11, like the terms of use.

2. Without an account, nothing is sent

The account-less Studio keeps everything in your browser (IndexedDB): your company, your customers, your catalogue, your history and your numbering. The online validator reads your file in your browser. Neither sends what you type or drop to any server, ours or a third party's. As long as you have no open session, the Studio does not even load the account code and does not contact Supabase.

One thing does go out, and it contains nothing about you. For each PDF produced in the Studio and each file checked in the validator, the page requests an address on our own site, with nothing attached. The server answers with no body and keeps only a log line, with the IP address truncated of its last byte. No file name, no verdict, no cookie, no identifier: it is a count, to know whether the tool is used.

This data stays with you, so we cannot recover it: if you clear the site's data in your browser, it is gone. Think of the Studio's backup.

Brevo receives your account's e-mail address and, for a reminder, the recipient's address, the subject, the text and the attached PDF. The site itself (pages, Studio, validator) is served by the publisher's server: it receives no account data, only page requests and their log.

Supabase is a US company. Your data is hosted in Paris, but hosting in Europe does not change the provider's nationality: Supabase Inc. remains subject to US law. We therefore cannot promise that your data escapes every request from a US authority addressed to that provider. If that is an obstacle for you, use the Studio without an account: nothing then leaves your browser.

No end-to-end encryption. Your data is encrypted at rest by the host, but it remains readable by the Service: the server reads your invoice to issue it, number it, work out what is still to be paid and send a reminder. That is the trade-off of having an account, and the choice is yours.

6. Your rights

You can access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict its use, object to a processing operation and take it away in a portable format. In the Studio, “Export my data” gives you a portable JSON file and all your PDFs, and account deletion follows the schedule in section 5. For anything else, write to the contact address given in section 1.

The customers whose data you entered exercise their rights with you, as the controller; we help you answer them (see the data processing agreement). Issued invoices fall under your legal retention obligation, which limits the right to erasure for that data.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can complain to the data protection authority of your country; in France, the CNIL.

7. Security

  • Every table in the database is protected by access rules that deny by default: one account can neither read nor write another's data.
  • Issuing an invoice and sending a reminder are done only by server functions. The browser has no write access to an issued invoice.
  • PDFs, logos and fonts sit in private storage. They open only through a signed link, valid for 60 seconds.
  • The database's service key exists only in the server functions' secrets; it is never sent to the browser.
  • The data is backed up daily by the host, and an encrypted copy is deposited every week with another European host.
  • Your sign-in session is kept in your browser's storage, so that you stay signed in. So that nothing you type is lost, the open draft is also copied into the browser.

The database schema, its access rules and the Service's functions are published in the project repository: you can read them.

8. Trackers and audience measurement

The site's pages embed no tracker and no third-party audience-measurement tool. Traffic is read from the access log of our own server, in which the IP address is truncated of its last byte as it is written, with GoAccess installed on that same server. The report is password-protected.
