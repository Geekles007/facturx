2. Without an account, nothing you enter is sent

2. Nothing you enter is sent

The account-less Studio keeps everything in your browser (IndexedDB): your company, your customers, your catalogue, your history and your numbering. The online validator reads your file in your browser. Neither sends what you type or drop to any server, ours or a third party's. As long as you do not open the sign-in, the Studio does not even load the account code and does not contact Supabase.

One thing does go out, and it carries neither what you entered nor your files. For each PDF produced in the Studio and each file checked in the validator, the page requests an address on our own site, with nothing attached. The server answers with no body, but the request leaves a line in two logs: the statistics log, where the IP address is truncated of its last byte, and the short-lived operating log, which keeps the full address (section 5). No file name, no verdict, no cookie: it is a count, to know whether the tool is used. The full IP address in the operating log is nonetheless personal data, which can identify your connection: that is why that log is short-lived, and the statistics log keeps only a truncated address.

This data stays with you, so we cannot recover it: if you clear the site's data in your browser, it is gone. Think of the Studio's backup.

6. Your rights

You can access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict its use, object to a processing operation and take it away in a portable format. In the Studio, “Export my data” gives you a zip archive with a portable JSON file and all your PDFs, and account deletion follows the schedule in section 5. For anything else, write Write to the contact address given in section 1.

The customers whose data you entered exercise their rights with you, as the controller; we help you answer them (see the data processing agreement). Issued invoices fall under your legal retention obligation, which limits the right to erasure for that data.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can complain to the data protection authority of your country; in France, the CNIL.

7. Security

  • Every table in the database is protected by access rules that deny by default: one account can neither read nor write another's data.
  • Issuing an invoice and sending a reminder are done only by server functions. The browser has no write access to an issued invoice.
  • Your PDFs, your logos and your fonts sit in private storage. They open only through a signed link, valid for 60 seconds.
  • The database's service key exists only in the server functions' secrets; it is never sent to the browser.
  • The Service does not, at present, make backup copies of the data: export yours regularly (“Export my data”).
  • Your sign-in session is kept in your browser's storage, so that you stay signed in. So that nothing you type is lost, the open draft is also copied into the browser.

The database schema, its access rules and the Service's functions are published in the project repository: you can read them.

8. Trackers and audience measurement

7. Trackers and audience measurement

The site's pages embed no tracker and no third-party audience-measurement tool. Traffic is read from the statistics log of our own server, in which the IP address is truncated of its last byte as it is written, with GoAccess installed on that same server. The report is password-protected.
